# Privacy and use of plate data

Number plates identify a vehicle's owner. Nepal's Individual Privacy Act (2018) restricts collecting,
storing and sharing such data.

- **Counting needs no plates.** Counts, speeds and congestion measures are anonymous and kept
  separate from enforcement output.
- **Plates are read only for flagged violations**, not for every vehicle; the system does not build
  a record of who drove where.
- **Enforcement use belongs with the traffic authorities.** Fines need legal authority, and every
  flagged case should be reviewed by a person before any action.
- **Nothing identifying is published.** No footage, plate image or plate dataset is committed to this
  repository; training runs in private notebooks. Blur plates and faces in any published image.
